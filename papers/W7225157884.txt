1. Introduction 

3 
 
 
The use of Artificial Intelligence is one of the main drivers of innovation 
in industrial engineering. The reason for this is that companies are under 
constant pressure to make better decisions more quickly in a rapidly 
changing environment. Artificial Intelligence can help with this by using 
real-time data to find hidden patterns and thus help decision-makers to 
make more informed decisions more quickly. This is particularly important 
in industrial environments, as decisions need to be taken quickly to avoid 
disruption to operations.[1] 
In cooperation with AI, intelligent agents work as self-determining aides 
that supervise given duties within industrial processes. Such agents keep a 
constant watch on different components of the system, carry out 
communication between agents, and coordinate their activities to ensure 
the smooth and safe running of operations.[2] They are capable of 
changing work routines on the spot, react quickly to any changes and less 
manual intervention. When used in conjunction, AI and intelligent agents 
help an industry switch its reactionary stance to a proactively and can 
greatly reduce downtimes, optimise resource utilisation, and increase 
overall reliability [3]. 
The Strong analytics of AI combined with the self-organized cooperation 
of intelligent agents, the industry becomes resilient. This enables 
companies to not onloperations. but also surpass efficiency and quality 
requirements, as well as keep up with high rate of changes in the market, 
and have a competitive advantage in a highly challenging world [1]. 
Technology AI and AI agents are transforming industry engineering 
because they lead to wise decisions and higher efficiency. They forecast 
machine malfunctions based on time-sensitive information [2] , minimize 
faults based on sophisticated quality inspection and optimize supply chains 
by dynamically managing the inventory and demand[3]. Collectively, AI 
and intelligent agents can enable industries to move on the scale of reactive 
problem-solving to proactive management, which smoothens operations, 
reduces costs and increases competitiveness.

3. Case Studies 
In this section two case studies have been used to demonstrate the practical 
implementation of AI and AI agents in industrial engineering. The first one 
talks about the real time predictive maintenance of industrial machinery. 
 
Case Study 1- Real-Time Predictive Maintenance Dashboard for 
Industrial Machines 
 
The industries today are always striving to maintain the operational 
efficiency of their machines at low cost. Traditional approaches such as 
repairing broken machines or following regular maintenance cycles prove 
to be less effective as these result into unplanned interruptions or 
unnecessary expenses [9]. The predictive maintenance, however, provides 
a much better alternative as it involves prediction of possible failure of 
machinery so that preventive repairs could be undertaken [11]. This study 
deals with the development of a Real time predictive Maintenance 
Dashboard which keeps a constant watch on the health of machines, 
identifies possible problems and alerts about these thorugh the live 
visualization [21]. Some of the major industries depend upon asset 
maintenance solutions such as IBM Maximo Application Suite (MAS) 
[22], Siemens MindSphere [23], GE Predix [24]. 
 
 These applications have various features; they can interact with IoT 
sensors, apply artificial intelligence algorithms to predict maintenance, and 
even manage maintenance process on the single platform. Nonetheless, 
each application presents its own difficulties. 
 
3.1 Proposed Solution: Real-Time Predictive Maintenance Dashboard 
 
In order to address this issue, a Real Time predictive Maintenance 
Dashboard will be developed, which will be light, flexible, and 
inexpensive. Rather than depending on heavy enterprise applications, a 
hybrid system for monitoring incoming machine data and the predicting 
failures with Random Forest, Isolation Forest and Autoencoder algorithms 
is proposed. [25] while Random Forest provides the probability of failure 
according to labeled patterns, Isolation Forest uncovers abnormal patterns

without labeling, and the Autoencoder algorithm pinpoints slight 
variations from normal operation. The results of the risk score and the

3.2 Machine learning simulation of Real-Time Monitoring 
 
A hybrid predictive maintenance solution is applied in order to forecast 
potential failures. In particular, the supervised algorithm is represented by 
a Random Forest Classifier model built with the help of Scikit-learn. It was 
used to estimate the likelihood of a failure occurrence, having the labelled 
data about the work of machines as input [25]. At the same time, for 
anomaly detection, an unsupervised Isolation Forest model and a deep 
Autoencoder neural network trained with TensorFlow/Keras were used. 
As a result, by combining all the models in one solution, the project 
succeeded in detecting familiar as well as previously unseen patterns of 
failures. To Simulate the real-time monitoring, the data about 
machines’work was provided to the system piece -by-peice with some 
artificial delay between the input data pieces using time module in python 
[30]. This way of processing the data resembles the real-life situation when 
data about the machine is transmitted from it. As a result, the hybrid model 
could run continuously, allowing early fault detection and more realistic 
monitoring of machine health.

Case Study 2 : AI Agent-Based Supply Chain Coordination for Sub-
Product Availability 
 
Supply chain coordination is essential in industrial engineering especially 
regarding the availability of the necessary sub-products that might include 
machinery, specialized tools, electronic components, or even semi-
intermediate raw materials [31]. The lack of these sub-products will lead 

23 
 
to disruption in production scheduling and increase costs in addition to 
affecting customers’ satisfaction [32]. Conventional supply chains depend 
largely on real-time data collection through IoT sensors and embedded 
devices which might not be feasible for small and mid-sized companies 
[33]. The lack of budget, system complexity or previous operations 
becomes an impediment to the IoT implementation in many cases. In such 
situation, artificial intelligence (AI) agents prove themselves to be an 
alternative solution, which helps improve coordination, forecasting, and 
decision making while not requiring real-time data from sensors. 
The presented case study is about the development of an AI-enabled 
coordination system to guarantee timely delivery of sub-products using 
historical demand data and available inventory data, without any help of 
the Internet of Things technology [34]. This solution is realistic and 
scalable, meeting the needs of real-life supply chains by means of smart 
processing of data. 
Many leading companies around the world rely on advanced supply chain 
management technologies such as SAP IBP [35], Oracle SCM Cloud, and 
Kinaxis Rapid Response for real-time inventory management and 
planning; however, the high cost, complexity, and dependence on the IoT 
or ERP platforms make them impractical for small or medium-sized 
businesses [36]. These limitations highlight the need for affordable, AI-
driven systems that can deliver similar efficiency without extensive 
technical or financial requirements [37].

4.Conclusion 

32 
 
From Supply chain optimization to the predictive maintenance and 
adaptive production planning, this paper has demonstrated how the 
development in artificial intelligence and intelligent agents is 
revolutionizing industrial engineering through smarter and more efficient 
decisions making processes. 
The Findings from the experiments carried out for both the cases are 
evidence of this revolutionary change brought about by the integration of 
AI. In the Real-Time Predictive Maintenance Dashboard, for example, 
machine failures were predicted beforehand, thus reducing unexpected 
downtimes and enhancing maintenance efficiency. In addition, the AI 
Agent-Based supply chain coordination system was able to increase the 
availability of sub-products due to accurate prediction of demand and 
effective stock management. 
With regard to the future developments in the Predicitve Maintenance 
System, there will be integration of IoT-based industrial sensors to collect 
and stream real time machine data into the dashboard. Communication and 
data synchronization will be done through cloud based APIs created via 
Fast API or Flask. Edge computing will be adopted near production floors. 
For the AI Agent-Based supply chain system, emphasis will be placed on 
integrating real-time inventory management by developing strong REST 
APIs and scaling up by deploying on cloud based platforms like AWS or 
Azure. Real time analytics at the edge will speed up decision making 
processes. In Subsequent versions, complex AI technologies like 
reinforcement learning and self-organizing multi-agent systems will be 
applied to make the system capable of adapting to unforeseen changes in 
demand, logistics or other external disturbances. 
Ultimately, combining AI with IoT and intelligent collaboration 
techniques marks a significant advance towards intelligent, self-
developing industrial ecosystems.
