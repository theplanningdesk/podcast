The Box by Levinson: Book Summary

The Box by Marc Levinson (2006) tells how the shipping container cut cargo handling costs and redrew the map of world trade. The episode covers the cost of handling, the deals with dock workers, the fight over standard sizes and the ports that won and lost, and what that means for network design today. For network designers, transport planners and anyone who wants to know why global supply chains look the way they do.
