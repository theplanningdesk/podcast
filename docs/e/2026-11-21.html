Financial Intelligence by Berman and Knight: Book Summary

Financial Intelligence by Karen Berman and Joe Knight explains why profit is an estimate and cash is the reality check. The episode covers the art of finance, profit against cash, working capital and the cash conversion cycle, and open-book management, and what each means for inventory planners and network designers. For planners who present plans to finance or own a budget.
